Tidy / Your controls

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

URL cleaning happens on your device. Tidy does not send your original or cleaned URLs to a Tidy server for cleaning, and has no advertising or analytics SDK.

This policy explains how Tidy and Tidy+ handle information. Tidy is developed by Arpit Anand. Some optional actions contact other services or send information to an app you choose, as described below.

Information on your device

  • URLs and shared text. Tidy processes URLs you enter, paste, share or select. When you enable clipboard checking, it reads available clipboard text for the cleaning features you choose. Shared message text is used for the current cleaning journey; History stores URL records, not the surrounding message.
  • History and statistics. Saved records contain the original URL, cleaned URL, domain, cleaning time and number of removed parameters. Tidy also keeps lifetime cleaning totals. URLs can contain personal information, access tokens or other sensitive details.
  • Settings and rules. Your preferences, custom rules, skipped domains and parameter exceptions are stored locally.
  • Diagnostics. Local crash reports can include the app version, Android version, device model and error trace. Error messages may contain information from the operation that failed. Reports are not uploaded automatically.
  • Tidy+ purchases. The Google Play edition stores signed purchase information locally to check access to paid features. This can include purchase identifiers and tokens. Tidy does not receive your payment-card details.

App data is stored in Android's app-private storage. History is not an encrypted vault, and Tidy does not provide a separate encryption password or cloud synchronisation service.

When information leaves your device

  • URL expansion. Expanding a short URL makes requests directly to the URL's server and any followed redirect destinations. Those services receive the requested URL, your IP address and normal request information. Expansion can contact a server before tracking parameters are removed. You can disable automatic short-URL expansion in Settings.
  • Rule updates. Tidy downloads public tracking rules from GitHub. The request does not include your cleaning History or input URLs. GitHub receives normal network request information, including your IP address.
  • Copy, Share and Open. Copy writes text to the system clipboard. Share sends text to the receiving app you choose, including through an automation tier you enable. Open hands the URL to another app, such as your browser. Those apps and destination services handle the data under their own policies.
  • Exports and backups. Exporting or sharing a backup sends the selected data to the destination you choose. Android system backup is off by default in Tidy. If you enable “Back up app data”, Android's backup provider can receive your app data. Local crash reports are excluded from system backup.
  • Reports and support. If you choose to submit an issue, send a crash report or contact the developer, the information you send reaches the selected service and the developer. GitHub issues are public. Review the contents and remove private URLs, tokens and personal information before sending.
  • Google Play. In the Google Play edition, billing and restore operations communicate with Google Play. Google handles purchase and payment information under its own policies.

Tidy does not sell your data or use it for advertising. The developer does not operate a server that receives your cleaning History.

Retention and deletion

Local History has no automatic expiry. You can clear History in the app. Its temporary Undo copy lasts only while the undo prompt is available; clearing History does not reset lifetime totals. You can delete local crash reports from About.

To remove all local app data, use Android's app storage controls. Uninstalling removes local app storage, but a system backup may later restore data. Copies you exported, shared or backed up are separate: delete them through the receiving app, storage location or backup provider. Turning off backup does not itself delete an earlier backup.

Information you voluntarily send to the developer is used to answer your enquiry or investigate the reported problem. Do not include information that is unnecessary for that purpose. You can request deletion through the channel you used to send it; the developer cannot delete copies held independently by GitHub, Google or another recipient.

Other services and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or third-party font requests. GitHub may process technical information when serving the pages. Its privacy statement explains that processing. Google's privacy policy applies to its services.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when Tidy's data handling changes, with the revision date shown above. For general privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public: do not include personal information, private URLs, purchase tokens or crash reports containing sensitive details. If you previously sent information privately, use that same channel for a deletion request.
